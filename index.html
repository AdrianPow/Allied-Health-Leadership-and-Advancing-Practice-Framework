Self-assessment tool · Office of the Chief Allied Health Officer · Health Workforce Division · Framework 2022

About this self-assessment

The Allied Health Leadership and Advancing Practice Framework helps you reflect on the clinical and non-clinical capabilities you need to advance your practice and your leadership. It describes how allied health professionals progressively expand their sphere of influence — from leading themselves, to leading others, to leading innovations, to leading systems.

This tool walks you through the framework's assessment process:

  1. Framework Assessment TreeA few short questions to identify which stage you are working within.
  2. Self-reflectionRate how strongly you agree with each statement for that stage.
  3. Capability CompassSee your average score in each of the six domains plotted visually.
  4. Supervisor reviewDiscuss your results, strengths and development areas with your supervisor.
  5. Work-Integrated Learning PlanRecord the development activities you will pursue.

Scores are not performance indicators. This is a reflective tool to help you and your supervisor identify strengths and target areas for development. Your answers stay in this browser — the app does not transmit your answers, notes or learning plan. Progress is saved on this browser profile until you discard it or clear browser data. Other people using the same profile may be able to access it. The government logo loads from Queensland Health when available. Opening a source link leaves this app.

The six domains

Leadership, research and communication are embedded within each of the six domains.

    The four stages

    Each stage builds on the previous one. The stages are not aligned to health practitioner (HP) levels — you do not need to be in a high-level role to progress through the framework.
